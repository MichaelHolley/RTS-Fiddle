# Total War reference

Controls, terms and mechanics copy Total War (Warhammer / Three Kingdoms era). New or changed mechanics match how Total War does it unless a deviation is listed below.

## Deliberate deviations

- **Split** ([controls](controls.md#split)): Total War has no in-battle split.
- **Join** ([controls](controls.md#join)): Total War only merges units in the campaign and caps the merged unit at full strength. Here units merge in battle with no cap.
- **Trees block soldiers** ([terrain](terrain.md#obstacles)): in Total War soldiers walk through the trees of a forest. Here every trunk is solid, as requested.
- **No pathfinding** ([terrain](terrain.md#moving-through-obstacles)): Total War routes units around impassable ground. Here units march straight and only their soldiers sidestep obstacles, which is enough while every obstacle is small and spaced apart.
- **Random maps** ([terrain](terrain.md#map)): Total War battle maps come from the campaign location. Here each load generates a new seeded map.
- **Player controls both sides** ([combat](combat.md)): a demo convenience; Total War gives the enemy to the AI.
- **No friendly fire or line of sight** ([combat](combat.md#ranged-fire)): bolts only damage their target and pass through hills, friends and obstacles.
- **No morale** ([combat](combat.md#health-and-casualties)): Total War units waver and rout; here they fight to the last soldier.
- **All-round fire** ([combat](combat.md#ranged-fire), [controls](controls.md#select)): Total War ranged units shoot within a forward arc and show it when selected; here soldiers turn to fire in any direction, so the range shows as a full circle.
- **Move keeps facing** ([controls](controls.md#move)): a Total War right-click turns units to face the direction of travel. Here they keep their facing and only a frontage order turns them, as requested.
- **Group march speed** ([formation](formation.md)): Total War matches speeds only for units the player has grouped. There is no grouping here, so every multi-unit move or frontage order marches at the slowest unit's speed.
