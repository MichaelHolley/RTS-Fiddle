# Total War reference

Controls, terms and mechanics copy Total War (Warhammer / Three Kingdoms era). New or changed mechanics match how Total War does it unless a deviation is listed below.

## Deliberate deviations

- **Split** ([controls](controls.md#split)): Total War has no in-battle split.
- **Join** ([controls](controls.md#join)): Total War only merges units in the campaign and caps the merged unit at full strength. Here units merge in battle with no cap.
